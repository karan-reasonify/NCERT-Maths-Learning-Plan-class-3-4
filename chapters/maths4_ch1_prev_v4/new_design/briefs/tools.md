## Tool 1: Blocks for Buildings  (M1.S1.T1.C2.2D1)

- **Approved purpose (unchanged):** The child picks wooden blocks for the roof, pillars and base of the buildings in the opening picture, builds the model and finds how it is like and unlike the real building.
- **Where:** right after the hook
- **One learning objective:** Students pick solid shapes for the roof, pillars and base of a building and compare the model with the real building.
- **Screen:** Frame as in every tool: progress dots on top, Back and Next at the sides, a Hint button. Book questions come one at a time, each in a small panel with its answer choices, such as cards, numbers or Yes and No. Every answer the tool shows starts with From the picture or Sample answer. PHASE 1, get ready: Diksha's mat from the story. Her wooden blocks lie in front of the India Gate drawing. One line says, Help Diksha build a model. Nothing is scored. PHASE 2, play: three buildings sit on top: India Gate, the sandy-brown gateway with tall minarets and the round thatched hut. Pick one. Drag blocks from the tray to a base pad, pillar pads and a roof pad. Every block in the tray can be used again and again. A block that cannot rest where it is put rolls or slides away, so you see which blocks stand firm. Tap Compare to put your model beside the real building. No block has a shape name written on it. PHASE 3, the six questions of your maths book: build the model, tap the parts you used (roof, pillars, base), pick why you chose them, pick a block for each part, then pick what is alike and what is different when the model sits beside the picture.
- **Controls:** Drag a block, Tap a block to turn it, Tap Compare, Reset
- **On-screen instruction:** Pick blocks for the roof, the pillars and the base. Build the model.
- **Visuals (same look across the chapter):** wooden_blocks, india_gate_drawing, other_buildings, diksha
- **Feedback:** A block on a flat pad stays. The pad names its part: base, pillars or roof., A block that cannot rest rolls or slides off. It says, This block rolls on its curved side., Compare puts your model beside the real building. Matching parts glow. Missing parts show, such as windows and colours., No try is wrong here. A different set of blocks makes a different model. Your answer may differ.
- **Done when:** A model stands with a base, pillars and a roof, Compare has been opened, and the six book questions are answered.
- **Questions (NCERT only, plus notice prompts on an exploration):**

  - [NCERT ch1.model1] Try to make a model of the buildings shown here using blocks.
  - [NCERT ch1.model_q1] What parts of the building have you shown in your model (for example, roof, pillars, base, etc.)?
  - [NCERT ch1.model_q2] Why did you select these parts?
