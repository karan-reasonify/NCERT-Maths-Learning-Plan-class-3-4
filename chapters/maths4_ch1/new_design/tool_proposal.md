## 6. Errors in the plan corrected (analysis 8C)

- Triangular pyramid "4 vertices meeting at the top apex" -> 4 corners in all; the three side faces meet at one of them, the apex.
- "Six connected squares = cube", "six rectangles = cuboid" (the book's own net A is six rectangles and does not fold), "a square and four triangles = square pyramid" -> never said as bare sentences; only valid nets are shown as figures, the four triangles joined to the four sides of the square.
- "Face is a flat surface" -> a face can be flat or curved (the book's cone and cylinder have a curved face); "edge" and "corner" are defined by pictures and the book's riddles, no cone corner is asked.
- Spin the Shape "How many faces can you see now?" -> only 1, 2 or 3 for a solid cube or cuboid; never offer 4 or more.
- Pentagon "regular" with equal sides -> equal sides do not give equal angles (the push test shows it); no "equal angles".
- Rigid triangle "does not easily change if side lengths remain fixed" -> it cannot change at all.
- Guess the Shape: clues 1 and 2 also fit a diamond; clue 3 decides.
- Lesson 1 mixing a flat word and a solid word ("looks like a triangle", "pyramid-like") -> a flat shape and a solid are named separately.
- Obtuse "smaller than 180" -> "more than a right angle" with no number; the book gives no upper limit (E28), Tool 8 shows only obtuse angles smaller than a straight line, except the book's dart (p23 shape 1, E22).
- Counts in the plan are all true (triangular prism 5, 9, 6; hexagonal prism 8, 18, 12; triangular pyramid 4, 6, 4; square pyramid 5, 8, 5; cube and cuboid 6, 12, 8; F+V-E = 2).
