## 1. The answer in one paragraph

No medium was added, dropped or moved: **1 hook video + 16 concept videos, 6 images (6 main, 0 pinned), 22 tools.** The count is
already at its floor. The flow gate needs one video or one image right before every tool after Tool 1, so 21 media items before 21
tools is the least possible; 5 of those 21 are images and 16 are videos. The only extra medium is the image after Tool 1 (T2.C1),
which holds six book items and no tool (CLASH 8). Fewer media would mean fewer tools (CLASH 2), which this step cannot decide. So
the work was the detail inside each choice: what each image shows and must not show, what each video poses and must leave to its
tool, and where one medium repeated another. No concept was turned into or out of an image concept (every key and role is as it
was). No concept has an image on top of a video or a tool; no concept is explained by video, image and tool all three.

| Count | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Hook video | 1 | 1 |
| Concept videos | 16 | 16 |
| Images, main flow | 6 | 6 |
| Images, pinned | 0 | 0 |
| Tools | 22 | 22 |

Pinned: none. Nothing here is a pure recap or reference of a concept a video or tool already teaches, and the platform's support for
`pinned_media_ids` is not verified (docs/02, notes on the wording). An image concept must be main in any case.
