## 2. Learning objectives (what the book is aiming at)

After this chapter a child can:

1. **build** a model of a building from blocks, boxes and cans, and **say** which shape stands for which part (roof, pillars, base) and how the model is like and unlike the real building (p1 to p2);
2. **name** prisms and pyramids, **find** the face shape common to all prisms (rectangle) and to all pyramids (triangle), and **explain** what a net is (p3);
3. **count** the faces (F), corners (V) and edges (E) of solids, **record** them in a table, **calculate** F+V−E and **explain** what they notice (p4);
4. **sort** solids by number of flat faces, number of straight edges, curved or flat faces, and with Venn circles (p4, p5, p6, p7);
5. **find** the face opposite a given face of a die or cube, and the faces that share an edge with a given face (p5);
6. **build** cube models, **count** the cubes in a tower and **draw** a cube on triangular dot paper (p7, p8);
7. **match** a description (faces, corners, edges) to a solid and **name** it; **match** nets to solids and **decide** which nets fold into a solid (p8, p9);
8. **identify** an angle as two lines that meet at a point, **mark** and **count** angles in pictures and objects (p10, p11);
9. **make** a right-angle checker from paper, **find** and **draw** right angles; **tell** acute (less than a right angle) from obtuse (more than a right angle) and **draw** and **mark** them (p11 to p13);
10. **test** with straws and clay whether a triangle, rectangle, square and pentagon keep their shape when pushed, and **describe** what happens to the angles (p14, p16);
11. **draw** three- and four-sided shapes on a dot grid, **count** their right angles and **compare** a rectangle and a square with other four-sided shapes (p15);
12. **make** a circle (equal straws, points at an equal distance from a point A), **name** centre, radius and diameter, **fold** paper to show that all diameters are equal and pass through the centre, and **state** that the diameter is double the radius (p16 to p18);
13. **use** a compass for a circle design, **mark** centre, radius and diameter on a design, and **compare** radii and diameters of wheels (p18, p19);
14. **solve** shape puzzles: hidden shapes, dividing shapes with lines or cuts, counting triangles and rectangles in a web, tracing a path that uses no wall twice, matchstick puzzles (p19 to p22);
15. **sort** 2D shapes by their sides, **classify** them by number of angles, **relate** number of sides to number of angles, and **identify** shapes by their kinds of angles (p20, p22, p23);
16. **find** which solid has a given number of straws (edges) and clay balls (corners) (p22).
