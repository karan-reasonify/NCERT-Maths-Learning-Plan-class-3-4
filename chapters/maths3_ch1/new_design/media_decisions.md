## What each image shows and must NOT show

All five are illustrations, not photos. Reason: the value of each picture is an exact letter, an exact dot or an exact count (the orange and blue letters, the 28-letter name, 12 children and six counts). A picture maker cannot promise those, and a photograph of real people or real boards cannot be made exact or be had as a file. Illustration lets the exact text be typeset over the picture and the exact counts be drawn.
