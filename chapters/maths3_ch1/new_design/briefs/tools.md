## Tool 1: Marks at the Gate  (M1.S1.T1.C2.2D1)

- **Approved purpose (unchanged):** Students make one mark for each cow that leaves and strike out one mark for each cow that returns. Include the textbook question: "Did all the cows come back home?"
- **Where:** right after the hook
- **One learning objective:** Students mark each cow that leaves, strike out one mark for each that returns, and tell if any cow is missing.
- **Screen:** FRAME, the same in every tool: a Hint lamp and a Next arrow. No score, no timer. One big picture: the cow shed gate with Deba and Deep beside it, and a low plain wall for the marks. A small herd of the plan's own waits in the shed (the builder uses 6 cows, with a small Practice tag; no number is ever shown or said). FIRST, GET READY (picks up the opening story): the cows wait quietly in the shed behind the gate. A thought bubble with a question mark floats over Deba and Deep, the problem of the story. Their friend comes to the wall and says in a speech bubble: 'A mark for each cow that goes out. Strike one out for each that comes in.' Two small pictures show it: a cow walking out beside a new mark, and a cow walking in beside a mark with a thin line across it. The child taps Next. THEN, PLAY. Morning: one cow walks to the gate and waits. The child taps Make a mark: a mark appears on the wall and the cow walks out. This repeats for every cow. Evening: the line changes to 'Strike out one mark for each cow that comes in.' A cow waits at the gate. The child taps Strike out a mark: a thin line crosses one mark and the cow walks in. The last cow is slow and is still on the path when the others are in, so for a moment one mark has no line. The three What do you notice? prompts show one at a time in a speech bubble: after the morning, after the first cow is in, and while the slow cow walks. They are read and thought about; nothing is typed. LAST, THE BOOK QUESTION: when the last cow is in, 'Did all the cows come back home?' shows under the wall with two big tiles, Yes and No. Its three steps show beside the wall; step 3 is 'Look at the wall. Does any mark have no line across it?'. The child looks at the wall: no mark is without a line, and that answers step 3. That leads to the book question, and the child taps Yes. Every mark now has a line across it.
- **Controls:** Tap Make a mark when a cow goes out, Tap Strike out a mark when a cow comes in, Tap the Yes tile or the No tile, Tap Reset to start again
- **On-screen instruction:** Mark each cow that goes out. Strike one out for each that comes in.
- **Visuals (same look across the chapter):** cow_shed, cows, tally_mark, struck_mark, deba_and_deep, wall_marks, their_friend
- **Feedback:** Right try: One cow goes out, so one mark appears on the wall., Right try: One cow comes in, so one mark gets a line across it., Right try: The last cow is on the path, so one mark has no line yet., Right try: Yes. Every mark has a line across it, so no cow is missing., Wrong try: No cow is at the gate yet. Wait for the next cow., Wrong try: This cow already has its mark. One mark for each cow., Wrong try: This cow is going out. Tap Make a mark, not Strike out a mark., Wrong try: This cow is coming in. Tap Strike out a mark, not Make a mark., Wrong try: Look at the wall again. Does any mark have no line? Every mark has a line, so no cow is missing., Tone, the same in every tool: a soft chime and 'Well done.' before a right reason; 'Not yet, try again.' before a wrong reason. A wrong try shakes gently and can be retried. No buzzer, no red cross, no score.
- **Done when:** First, the child has read the friend's idea and tapped Next. Then, every cow has gone out with one mark made and has come in with one mark struck out. Last, the child has tapped Yes for the book question and the wall shows every mark with a line across it.
- **Questions (NCERT only, plus notice prompts on an exploration):**
  - [notice] What do you notice about the marks on the wall?
  - [notice] What happens to a mark when a cow comes back?
  - [notice] How can the marks show you that a cow is missing?
  - [NCERT ch1.story2] Did all the cows come back home?
- **Hints:**
  - orienting: Look at the gate. Is the cow going out or coming in?
  - strategic: One cow, one mark. Going out: make a mark. Coming in: strike one out.
  - specific: A cow is waiting at the gate. Tap the button that matches what it does.
  - bottom-out: Cow going out: tap Make a mark. Cow coming in: tap Strike out a mark. At the end, look for a mark with no line.

## Tool 5: Starting and Ending Letters  (M2.S4.T4.C10.2D1)

- **Where:** after the concept video it applies to (M2.S4.T4.C9)

- **Screen:** FRAME, the same in every tool: a Hint lamp and a Next arrow. No score, no timer.
