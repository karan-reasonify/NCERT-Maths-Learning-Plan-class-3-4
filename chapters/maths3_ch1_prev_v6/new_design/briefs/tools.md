## Tool 1: Marks at the Gate  (M1.S1.T1.C2.2D1)

- **Approved purpose (unchanged):** The child makes one mark for each cow that leaves the gate and strikes one out for each cow that comes back, then sees whether any cow is missing.
- **Where:** right after the hook
- **One learning objective:** Students make one mark for each cow that leaves, strike one out for each that returns, and notice whether any cow is missing.
- **Screen:** Same frame as every tool (step dots, Hint, Reset, Back, Next; no score, no timer). One big picture: the cow shed gate with Deba and Deep beside it, and a plain wall for the marks. Eight cows (the plan's own practice herd, with a small label Practice) wait in the shed. No number is shown on screen. PHASE 1, get ready: one line, 'Deba and Deep want to know that no cow is lost.' PHASE 2, play. Morning: a cow walks to the gate and waits. The child taps Make a mark, one upright bar appears on the wall and the cow walks out. This repeats for every cow. When the last cow is out, the first notice prompt shows. Evening: the line changes to 'Strike out one mark for each cow that comes back.' A cow walks to the gate and waits. The child taps Strike out a mark, a slanting line crosses the top upright bar and the cow walks in. This repeats for every cow, and the second notice prompt shows after the first cow is in. PHASE 3, the book's question: when the last cow is in, the question 'Did all the cows come back home?' shows under the wall with two big answer tiles, Yes and No. The child looks at the wall and taps one tile. The third notice prompt shows last. The three notice prompts are read and thought about: no answer is entered, the child taps Next.
- **Controls:** Make a mark, Strike out a mark, Tap the Yes tile or the No tile, Reset
- **On-screen instruction:** Make a mark when a cow goes out. Strike one out when it comes back.
- **Visuals (same look across the chapter):** tally_mark, struck_mark, wall_marks, cow_shed, cows, deba_and_deep
- **Feedback:** Right try: One cow goes out, so one mark appears on the wall., Right try: One cow comes in, so one mark is struck out., Right try: Yes. - **Done when:** Every cow has gone out with one mark made and has come back with one mark struck out, and the child has tapped Yes or No for the book's question.
- **Questions (NCERT only, plus notice prompts on an exploration):**

  - specific: A cow is waiting at the gate. Tap the button that matches what it is doing.
  - bottom-out: Going out: tap Make a mark. Coming in: tap Strike out a mark.
