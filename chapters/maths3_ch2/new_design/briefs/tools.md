## Tool 1: Explore Shapes Around Us  (M1.S1.T1.C2.2D1)

- **Approved purpose (unchanged):** Explore a ball, dice, bottle, shoebox and birthday cap. Rotate and observe, match similar objects and notice which can roll, slide or stand.
- **Where:** right after the hook
- **One learning objective:** Students group everyday objects that look alike and notice which ones roll, slide or stand.
- **Screen:** A bright table seen from the front. The five toys from the story stand on it, large and shaded. They are a football, a die, a shoebox, a bottle and a birthday cap. A tray under the table holds five look-alike partners. They are a marble, a cube-shaped box, an eraser, a chalk stick and a wooden cone. These object names are for the builder only. Every toy and every partner is shown as an UNLABELLED picture tile: no word is written on or under any tile, so no solid name reaches the child. No shape name is written anywhere. Only the words rolls, slides and stands appear, after a push or a try. A push shows what the object does the way it lies now. A football or a marble rolls every way. A bottle or a chalk stick lying down rolls one way. A cap or a wooden cone lying down rolls round and round. A die, a cube-shaped box, a shoebox and an eraser slide. Any object standing upright just slides when nudged.
- **Controls:** Drag to turn an object, Tap Push, Tap Stand it up, Drag a partner next to its toy
- **On-screen instruction:** Turn, push and stand each toy. Put the ones that look alike together.
- **Visuals (same look across the chapter):** football, die, shoebox, bottle, birthday_cap, marble, cube_box, eraser, chalk, wooden_cone
- **Feedback:** Alike pair: the partner glows beside its toy. Push both and they move the same way., Not alike: the partner slides back to the tray. Turn both and look at every side again., Push result: rolls every way, rolls one way, rolls round and round, or slides., Stand result is one of these. Stands on a flat side. Stands on a flat end. Stands on its round base. Will not stand.
- **Done when:** Each toy is turned, pushed and stood up, and each partner is matched.
- **Questions (NCERT only, plus notice prompts on an exploration):**
  - [notice] Turn each object around. What do you notice from every side?
  - [notice] Which objects look alike? Why do you put them together?
  - [notice] Push each one. Which roll, slide or stand? Do the rolling ones all move the same way?
- **Hints:**
  - orienting: Turn each object. Look at it from every side.
  - strategic: Find two objects that look alike. Push them both.
  - specific: Push the football and the marble. Do they move in the same way?
  - bottom-out: The marble looks like the football. Drag the marble next to the football.

- **Controls:** Drag to turn the solid, Pick Face, Edge or Corner, Tap or trace the part on the solid, Drag a solid or a card to a row
- **On-screen instruction:** Pick Face, Edge or Corner. Then tap or trace it on the solid.
- **Visuals (same look across the chapter):** face_edge_corner_figure, cube, cuboid, cylinder, cone, sphere, simple_house
- **Feedback:** Right part: it glows and says its name, such as curved edge., Not that part: it stays dim and says what it is. - **Hints:**
