## M1.S1.T1.C1.VID1  The Mystery of Toys (hook video, about 75 s)

- **Purpose:** Jaya opens a toy box and finds a football, a die, a shoebox, a bottle and a birthday cap. Some roll and some stand. The story ends on the open question why, names no solid shape and leaves the answer to Tool 1.
- **Concept it explains:** none (hook)
- **Characters:** Jaya, Devika, the children
- **Setting:** the classroom craft corner: a long table and a big toy box
- **What happens:** It is the day before the toy show. Jaya opens the toy box. She takes out a football, a die and a shoebox. Then she takes out a bottle and a birthday cap. Devika lines them up on the long table. Jaya gives each toy a gentle push. The football rolls off the edge and the children chase it. The bottle, lying down, rolls a little way. The cap, on its side, rolls round and round. The die and the shoebox only slide a short way and stop. Then the children try to stand each toy up. The bottle, the die, the shoebox and the cap all stand. The football will not stay on one spot, however hard Jaya tries. Nobody says what any toy is called or why it moves that way.
- **Ends on the open question:** Why does the football roll away, while the shoebox stays where it is?
- **Concept:** The Mystery of Toys (M1.S1.T1.C1)

## M1.S2.T2.C3.VID1  Meet the Solid Shapes (concept video, about 120 s)

- **Purpose:** Jaya and Devika line up the football, die, shoebox, bottle and birthday cap from the toy box, and the children give each kind of solid shape its name.
- **Concept it explains:** the five solid shapes and their names: cube, cuboid, cylinder, cone and sphere
- **Characters:** Jaya, Devika, the children
- **Setting:** the craft corner, with a shelf and blank name cards for the toy show
- **What happens:** The toy show opens tomorrow. Jaya and Devika must put a name card on each shelf. All the cards are blank. The children show the craft they made. A puppy face is on a bottle. A cat face is on the shoebox. A pig face is on a box like the die. A bird face is on the birthday cap. Jaya holds up the toys one at a time.
- **The problem:** Devika cannot tell which name goes with which toy. The cards stay blank until the children name each kind. So the children name each kind. Devika writes each name on a card. A ball is a sphere. A die is a cube. A shoebox is a cuboid. A bottle is a cylinder. A birthday cap is a cone. The football is drawn as a full, shaded ball. Then Devika lays the bottle on its side. She says it is a new kind of toy and needs a new card. Jaya stands the bottle up and lays it down again. Then Jaya holds the cap with its tip down. She lays it on its side. It is the same bottle and the same cap. So each keeps its one name.
- **Story thread (how it continues):** The toys from the hook are on the shelf. The children have pushed them, turned them and stood them up. Now they need names for them.
- **Continues from:** M1.S1.T1.C1.VID1
- **Leads into:** Tool 2, Meet the 3D Shapes. The child turns each solid and names it. Then the child matches it to an everyday object.
- **Concept:** Meet the Solid Shapes (M1.S2.T2.C3)

## M1.S3.T3.C5.VID1  The Secret Parts of Shapes (concept video, about 130 s)

- **Purpose:** Jaya looks closely at the toy solids and finds the faces, edges and corners of each.
- **Concept it explains:** the parts of a solid: faces, edges and corners, and a face is flat or curved
- **Characters:** Jaya, Devika
- **Setting:** the craft corner, with a gift box being decorated for the toy show
- **What happens:** Devika decorates a gift box shaped like the die. She wants a ribbon on the lines where two sides meet. She wants a star on each pointed place. She wants paint on each flat side. But she only says that bit and points. Jaya cannot tell which bit she means. Jaya fetches a big magnifying glass and looks closely at the box. Close-ups show a flat side lighting up. A line where two sides meet glows. A pointed place sparkles. Together they find the names. A flat side is a face. The line is an edge. The pointed place is a corner. Then Jaya looks at the bottle. Its top and bottom are flat. Its side bends all the way round. That side is a curved face. The rim, where the flat top meets the side, is an edge too. It is a curved edge. The football is one curved face all over. It has no lines and no pointed places.
- **The problem:** The friends have no words for the parts of a solid. So the ribbon, the stars and the paint go to the wrong places. Devika also wonders if a curved side counts, because it is not flat.
- **Story thread (how it continues):** The five solids now have names on the shelf cards. The die is a cube. Now the children need names for its parts.
- **Continues from:** M1.S2.T2.C3.VID1
- **Leads into:** Tool 3, Explore Faces, Edges and Corners. The child taps a face, traces an edge and finds a corner.
- **Concept:** The Secret Parts of Shapes (M1.S3.T3.C5)

## M2.S5.T5.C9.VID1  Let's Build Something! (concept video, about 120 s)

- **Purpose:** Jaya wants to build a house, a rocket or a tower and tells Devika where each shape goes.
- **Concept it explains:** making a model by arranging solid shapes and telling where each shape is placed
- **Characters:** Jaya, Devika, the children
- **Setting:** two tables in the craft corner, with a cardboard screen standing between them
- **What happens:** For the toy show, Jaya builds a little house on her table. The dice stand side by side, with a gap between them. The shoebox lies on top of the dice. The birthday cap stands on its round base. It sits on top of the shoebox as the roof. The show needs the same house on Devika's table. Devika must build it without seeing Jaya's. Jaya first just names the pieces: the dice, the shoebox and the cap. Devika's house comes out wrong. Her shoebox is at the bottom, and the dice sit on top of it. The cap lies on its side beside the shoebox. So Jaya starts again from the bottom. She says where each piece goes, one piece at a time. She uses the words on top of, under and between. Now the two houses match. The children roll a marble through the gap under the shoebox. The story builds in order and never counts the pieces of a finished model.
- **The problem:** Saying only what the pieces are is not enough. Devika's house comes out wrong. Jaya needs words for the order and the place of each piece.
- **Story thread (how it continues):** The children know the names and the parts of the solids. They have looked closely at the rocket, the house and the engine. Now they build a model of their own and tell a friend how.
- **Continues from:** M1.S3.T3.C5.VID1
- **Leads into:** Tool 5, Build and Describe. The child builds a model and says where each shape goes. Then the child builds one from a friend's words.
- **Concept:** Let's Build Something! (M2.S5.T5.C9)

## M2.S6.T6.C11.VID1  Building with Clay, Sticks and Cubes (concept video, about 100 s)

- **Purpose:** The children join clay balls with sticks to make a frame, then join cubes to make a bigger shape, showing the joining only, with no count, no hidden cubes and no meaning given to balls and sticks.
- **Concept it explains:** a solid shape can be made by joining parts: balls and sticks make a frame, cubes make a bigger solid
- **Characters:** Jaya, Devika, the children
- **Setting:** the craft corner at the end of a busy day
- **What happens:** The toy show shelf is full. The old boxes are all used up. The children still want a big toy for the middle of the table. Jaya finds clay, a bundle of thin sticks and a pile of small cubes. She rolls clay into balls. She presses a stick into two balls to join them. She keeps joining until she has a see-through frame. The frame stands on the table. Devika joins small cubes face to face to make a longer block. Then she makes a bigger shape that stands by itself. The children set the frame and the cube shape in the middle. The story shows the joining only. It counts nothing and gives no meaning to the balls or the sticks.
- **The problem:** There are no old boxes left. The children need a big shape for the middle of the table. They find that small parts can be joined to make one.
- **Story thread (how it continues):** In the last story the children built models by stacking toys. Now the toys have run out. So they make new shapes by joining small parts.
- **Continues from:** M2.S5.T5.C9.VID1
- **Leads into:** Tool 6, Build with Clay, Sticks and Cubes. The child joins clay balls and sticks, then joins cubes.
- **Concept:** Building with Clay, Sticks and Cubes (M2.S6.T6.C11)
